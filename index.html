What we collect

We collect the information you provide in order to run the service:

  • Account information — the name and email address you use to sign in. You may sign in with email and password or with your Google account.
  • Property management data you enter — properties, units, tenants (including tenant names, email addresses and phone numbers), leases and their terms, rent-reminder records and payment status, maintenance requests and their triage/review results, repair-schedule data, communications drafts and sent messages, and other records you create in the app.
  • Documents and photos — files you upload (for example a lease or a property photo), which are stored on our behalf by our hosting/database provider.
  • AI feature configuration — which AI features you have enabled and, if you choose to provide one, your AI provider API key. Provider keys are encrypted at rest and never returned to your browser.

We do not collect precise location, contacts from your device address book, device identifiers for advertising, health data, or payment-card details. The app ships no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK.

How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, and to scope data so only you can see it.
  • To operate the features you use: managing properties and tenants, tracking leases and maintenance, drafting and sending tenant communications, scheduling repairs, and generating reminders.
  • To provide AI assistance (see “AI features” below).
  • To deliver communications you ask us to send by email.

AI features and what is shared with AI providers

Some features offer AI assistance. These calls run on our servers and only one AI provider, OpenAI, is called from the application. When a feature is turned off, or no provider credential is configured, the feature falls back to a deterministic on-device/server calculation and nothing is sent to an AI provider. The only situations in which data is sent to OpenAI are:

  • Maintenance triage — the maintenance issue's title, description and category are sent to classify urgency, cost, safety, and contractor specialty. This generally does not include personal information.
  • Lease analysis — if you run a lease risk analysis, the full lease text you supply is sent to OpenAI. Because it is your document, it may contain tenant names, addresses, and other personal or financial details. Only use lease analysis with documents you are authorised to process.
  • Communications drafting — the tenant's name, unit, and property name are sent so the assistant can draft a message in the tone you choose. Your drafted message is always shown to you for review before it is sent.
  • Repair scheduling — is calculated locally on our servers and sends nothing to an AI provider.

AI results are assistance only: they are not a substitute for your own judgment, review, or professional advice, and they are not guaranteed to be accurate.

Third-party services we share data with

Your data is shared only with the providers needed to run the service, and only to the extent required:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, row-level security, and file storage. This is where your account and business records are stored. (supabase.com/privacy)
  • OpenAI — AI assistance, as described above. (openai.com/privacy)
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with your Google account. (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Resend — the email provider that delivers messages you ask the app to send (tenant communications and rent reminders) to the recipient's email address. (resend.com/privacy)

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. AI provider keys are sent only to the provider you configured, during an AI call, and only as an authentication header.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) for traffic between the app and our providers. AI provider API keys are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) before storage. Other business data is stored with the platform's standard storage encryption. Data access is scoped to your account through row-level security. We use a Content Security Policy and restrict browser permissions such as camera, microphone, and geolocation.
