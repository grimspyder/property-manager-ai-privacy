What deleting an account removes

Deleting your account permanently and immediately removes: your profile and sign-in account, properties, units and tenant records, leases and rent reminders (including any payment status you recorded), maintenance requests and AI triage/review results, repair-scheduling data, drafted and sent tenant communications, documents and every file uploaded to storage, and stored AI provider credentials and settings. Deleted data cannot be recovered by you or by support.

If you can still sign in — delete directly

The fastest and most complete way to delete your account is in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. You will type a confirmation and, for email/password accounts, your password, and the deletion completes immediately. Sign in to reach Settings.

If you cannot sign in — request deletion

If you can no longer access your account, complete the form below. It will open your mail app with a pre-filled deletion request addressed to johnsoler.se@gmail.com. An operator verifies the request manually and confirms once your data is removed. This request does not itself delete anything automatically.

Requests are handled in the order received; if you do not hear back within see the notes in this document, follow up by replying to the confirmation email.

How to delete your account

Inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm (email/password users also re-enter their password). This permanently removes your properties, units, tenants, leases, maintenance requests, communications, reminders, documents (including stored files), scheduling data, and AI configuration, and then deletes your sign-in account.

Without the app: email johnsoler.se@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will verify and delete your account and data.
